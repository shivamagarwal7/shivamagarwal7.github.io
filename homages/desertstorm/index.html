The Long Way Home

Royal Enfield, Rider Mania, Goa, and the Classic 500 Desert Storm. An animated love letter to riding.

A Royal Enfield on a coastal road. Rider Mania after sundown. The matte sand of a Classic 500 Desert Storm.

This homage brings them together through illustrated scenes and slow, cinematic motion: a little corner for the machines, places, and people that make riding special.

Explore the ride, the gathering, and the bike. Pause the animation whenever you want, or let the scenes unfold as you scroll.
